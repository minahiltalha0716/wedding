~The Golden Era ✨~

90's Mehndi

The 90s desi wedding was its own world 🌸. Dholki till midnight, marigolds everywhere, fairy lights that flickered but stayed up anyway, and all the women gathered around the bride humming tapay on dholak ki thap, sending her off to her new home wrapped in love. Mehndi nights weren’t planned to perfection, they just happened. Loud, chaotic, and somehow always beautiful. We’re bringing that energy back.
